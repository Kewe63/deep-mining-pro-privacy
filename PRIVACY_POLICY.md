# Privacy Policy — Deep Mining Pro

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026  
**App version:** 1.0.0

Publisher identity for this release package: **Deep Mining Pro**. Privacy and support requests can be submitted to the monitored email address listed below. The public policy is published at `https://kewe63.github.io/deep-mining-pro-privacy/` and the same support email must be configured in Play Console.

## Summary

Deep Mining Pro stores game progress and settings locally on the user's device. The app does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, account registration, or a developer backend for gameplay data in this release. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing.

## Data stored on the device

The app may store the following locally in browser/WebView storage:

- saved world and mining progress;
- settings such as language, sound, graphics, and controls;
- achievements and collection progress;
- point balance and verified purchase receipt identifiers needed to prevent duplicate crediting.

This local information is used only to operate the game, restore progress, and preserve paid entitlements. It is not transmitted to a developer server by the current app implementation.

## Google Play purchases

If the user buys a point pack, Google Play processes the transaction. The app receives purchase information needed to fulfill the consumable item, such as product ID, purchase token, and/or Google Play order ID. The app records the minimum receipt identifier and credited amount locally so the same order is not granted twice. Purchase tokens are consumed through Google Play only after the credit is durably saved.

Google processes payment and purchase data under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive full payment-card details.

## Network access

Network access may be used for Google Play Billing and for downloading a separately generated APK when the web edition's download control is used. The release does not claim that a placeholder URL is live; the publisher must configure the final download and privacy-policy URLs before distribution.

## Data sharing

The app does not sell user data. The current release does not share gameplay data with advertising or analytics companies. Purchase processing necessarily involves Google Play as the payment platform.

## Retention and deletion

Gameplay reset controls remove or regenerate gameplay progress as described in the confirmation message while preserving paid balance and verified purchase history to protect purchased value and prevent duplicate fulfillment. Users who want to remove all locally stored app data can clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Google Play may retain transaction records independently under Google's policies.

## Security

Purchase fulfillment is designed to record the paid credit before consuming the Google Play purchase token. Release signing credentials must remain outside the repository and must not be embedded in the app or build scripts.

## Children

The publisher must confirm the intended age group in the Play Console Target Audience and Content questionnaire. If the app is directed to children, the publisher must obtain appropriate legal review and update this policy and product configuration before release.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating this document's effective date and the published policy artifact.
